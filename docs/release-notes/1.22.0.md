# TCG Binder 1.22.0

Buy it, receive it, done — the full acquisition flow.

## Added

- **Received, per purchase**: every purchase in transit gets its own "Received" button. One tap registers the arrival — the copies enter a collection (price paid recorded), the deck takes them, and *that* purchase shrinks by exactly what came. Bought 1 from three different sellers and only one package arrived? The other two stay on the way, correctly attributed.
- **Partial receipts**: the receive dialog's quantity is editable (capped at that purchase's size) — a package with 1 of 3 copies leaves the purchase open with the remaining 2.
- **"I have it"**: for copies the plugin already counts as free, one tap allocates them to the deck; copies it didn't know about go through the same quick add-to-collection flow.
- **Purchases with price and date**: each purchase line now takes an optional per-copy price and a purchase date (defaults to today).
- **Price paid on collection entries** (`paid`, per copy): stamped on receipt, shown as a "Paid" column (only when present), merged as a quantity-weighted average when batches combine.
- **Purchases on the way** section on the dashboard: every in-transit purchase across all decks — card, seller, price, date, owning deck — with Receive and Cancel actions plus "on the way" / "still to buy" totals.

## Changed

- **The wishlist now means "still to buy"** and keeps itself true: marking copies as bought removes them from the wishlist, cancelling a purchase puts them back, and "I have it"/"Received" clear them too.
- The wishlist's ✓ action opens the same receive dialog — with an optional "belongs to deck" choice so the arrived copies can go straight into a deck.
- Missing-list rows carry a state chip (*To buy* / *On the way*) and per-seller Receive buttons instead of the old "+" that always consumed the oldest purchase.
